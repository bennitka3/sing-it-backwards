The Song Vault

Every song here is a card on Room 2's wall. Nursery songs come with a built-in tune. For the pop songs, record yourself singing the first 7 seconds of the chorus. Then put the songs on the site so every player has them, with nothing to download.

Songs

Put the songs on the site

  1. Press Save song pack. It saves pack.json to your Downloads.
  2. Open the repo's upload page, drag pack.json in, and press Commit changes.
  3. About a minute later, every Room 1 has these songs. Players download nothing.

The site is public, so only put your own singing in the pack, never a copy of the real record.
