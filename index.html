A two-room game, inspired by the backwards-song challenge on The Traitors. Room 1 hears a song played in reverse and copies the sounds. Room 2 only hears that recording, flipped the right way round, and has to find the song on the wall.

How to play

  1. Everyone joins your usual video call. Nothing to install and no logins.
  2. The host presses Start a new game and sends the Room 1 link to the singers and the Room 2 link to the guessers.
  3. Room 1 draws a secret song (the famous first line, about 7 seconds), plays it backwards (3 times unless you change it above), records themselves copying the backwards sounds and presses Send.
  4. The recording pops up on every open Room 2 page. They play it flipped (2 times unless you change it). Done well, the gibberish turns back into the song.
  5. Room 2 pins one card on the wall, and the page says whether they got it right.

Headphones help in Room 1, so the backwards song doesn't leak into the recording. Recordings are kept for the game in a small free database (Google Firebase) and nowhere else.
