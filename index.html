A two-room game, inspired by the backwards-song challenge on The Traitors. Room 1 hears a song played in reverse and copies the sounds. Room 2 only hears that recording, flipped the right way round, and has to find the song on the wall.

Room 1

The Singer

Listen to a song in reverse, sing it back, send it on. Room 2

The Wall

Hear the flipped recording and pin the right card.

How to play

  1. Everyone joins your usual video call and opens this site in a browser. Nothing to install.
  2. Optional: the host opens Host, adds clips for the pop songs and sends the song pack file to Room 1. Without it, Room 1 gets the nursery songs.
  3. Room 1 draws a secret song, plays it backwards as often as they like, records themselves copying the backwards sounds and saves the recording.
  4. Room 1 drops that file in the team chat. Room 2 opens it on their page and plays it flipped. Done well, the gibberish turns back into the real song.
  5. Room 2 pins one card on the wall. The answer is hidden inside the file, so the page can say whether they got it right.

Headphones help in Room 1, so the backwards song doesn't leak into the recording. Nothing is uploaded: songs and recordings stay on your computers and in your chat.
